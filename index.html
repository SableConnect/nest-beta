Questions

Parents ask

What does it cost?

Nest is free for families, with no ads. Later there may be optional extras, but anything that helps you look after your child stays free.

Who can see my child's messages?

You and any other parent in your family, at the level you choose for your child's age. A friend's parents see their own child's chats with yours, at their own setting. Your child is told what applies.

Can my child join without me?

No. A parent creates the family and links each child's phone with a one-time code from the parent's account.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone and Android. Tell us above which your family uses, so your invite has the right steps.

Does it replace WhatsApp?

For your child's chats with family and approved friends, yes. WhatsApp's own parent controls let you manage contacts but not see messages. Nest lets you see as much as their age needs.
