Install help walks you through it in 3 minutes

Which phone are you installing it on?

iPhoneTakes about 3 minutes AndroidTakes about 3 minutes

iPhone

Nest is still in testing, so on iPhone it comes through Apple's free testing app, TestFlight.

  1. Install TestFlightTap the button, then tap Get in the App Store.
    Get TestFlight
  2. Open the Nest invitationCome back to this page and tap the button. TestFlight opens.
    Open the Nest invitation
  3. Send us your Apple emailReply to your invite email (or our WhatsApp message) with the Apple email signed in on that iPhone (open Settings and tap the name at the top to see it). For a child's iPhone, send the Apple email on the child's iPhone. Within a day you'll get an email from TestFlight called "Nest". Open it on the iPhone and tap View in TestFlight.
  4. Install NestIn TestFlight, tap Accept, then Install. Nest appears on your home screen.
  5. Allow notificationsWhen you first open Nest, tap Allow, so you know when a message or a call comes in.

Updates arrive by themselves. If TestFlight ever says a new version is ready, open TestFlight and tap Update.

Android

Nest is in early access on Google Play.

  1. Open Nest on Google PlayTap the button. If Google Play asks, tap Become a tester, then download it on Google Play.
    Get Nest on Google Play
  2. InstallTap Install. Nest appears with your other apps.
  3. Open Nest and allow notificationsTap Open, then Allow when Nest asks to send notifications.

Updates arrive through Google Play, like any other app.

Nest isn't in the Play Store yet, so you install it straight from our download link. Your phone will ask you to allow this once.

  1. Download NestTap the button. If your phone asks, tap Download (the file is about 100 MB, so Wi-Fi is best).
    Download Nest for Android
  2. Open the fileWhen the download finishes, tap Open in the message at the bottom of the screen, or open your notifications and tap the Nest download.
  3. Allow the install, onceIf your phone says it can't install apps from this source, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then tap the back arrow.
  4. InstallTap Install. If Google Play Protect warns that the app is unknown, tap More details, then Install anyway. (It warns because Nest isn't in the Play Store yet.)
  5. Open Nest and allow notificationsTap Open, then Allow when Nest asks to send notifications.

Updates arrive by themselves: when you close and reopen Nest, it picks up the newest version.

Setting up your family

One parent does this first, on their own phone.

  1. Parent: create your familyOpen Nest and tap I'm a parent. Sign up with the email your invite came to and a password, read the consent box and tick I agree, then tap Create family.
  2. Add your childrenGo to Settings, type your child's first name and age, and tap Add and get a link code. You get a 6-digit code.
  3. Link your child's phoneInstall Nest on your child's phone the same way. Open it, tap I have a family code and type the 6-digit code. Your child reads a short note about what you can see and taps I understand.
  4. Add the other parentIn Settings, add them as a parent to get a code. They sign up on their phone and tap Join your partner's family.
  5. Connect with usTo let our children chat, swap friend codes with us (shown on each child's home screen). Both families' parents say yes before the children can chat.
  6. Invite friends' familiesIn Contacts, tap Share my invite link. A family who asks through your link usually gets their invite straight away.

Good to know

  • Nest is in early testing, so things may change and the odd thing may not work. Please tell us what's confusing or broken: a screenshot helps a lot.
  • Your messages are stored on Nest's servers in Europe and aren't yet end-to-end encrypted.
  • Children can report anything that worries them to you or, privately, to Nest's safety team.
  • You can delete your family and everything in it at any time, in Settings.
